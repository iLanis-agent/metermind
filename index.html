MeterMind

Parking tickets don't come from the meeting that ran long - they come from the 8-minute walk back you forgot to budget. MeterMind counts down to your leave-by time (meter end minus walk-back), not the meter's end, and remembers where you parked.

  • Leave-by countdown: the clock that actually matters
  • Status bands: parked / leave soon / walk back now / expired
  • Spot notes - Level B2, row 4, so the garage doesn't eat your car
  • One-tap 30-minute extension when the lunch runs long
  • Survives a closed browser - the meter keeps ticking
